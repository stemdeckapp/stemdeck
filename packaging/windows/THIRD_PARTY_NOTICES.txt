Downloaded During First-Run Setup
---------------------------------

FFmpeg binaries are not bundled in the StemDeck alpha zip. They are downloaded
during first-run setup. FFmpeg builds may be distributed under LGPL or GPL
terms depending on how they are compiled. StemDeck should display the selected
FFmpeg build source and license before or during download.

Demucs model weights are not bundled in the StemDeck alpha zip. They are
downloaded during first use. StemDeck should display the model source and
license/usage terms before or during download.

Separation model weights are not bundled either. They are downloaded when a
song first uses them, into the models folder, and are not redistributed by
StemDeck:

- Mel-Band RoFormer vocals model by Kimberley Jensen (the Kim model). License:
  MIT, as its model card declares. StemDeck downloads one pinned revision and
  checks its SHA-256. Source: https://huggingface.co/KimberleyJSN/melbandroformer
  It runs through audio-separator (MIT).
- Demucs fine-tuned models (htdemucs_ft), from the same Demucs project as the
  Demucs entry above. License: MIT (Meta/Facebook Research).
  Source: https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs
- ByteDance high-resolution piano transcription weights
  (CRNN_note_F1=0.9677_pedal_F1=0.9186.pth, "High-resolution Piano
  Transcription with Pedals by Regressing Onsets and Offsets Times", by
  Qiuqiang Kong, 2020). License: CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source:
  https://zenodo.org/records/4034264 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.4034264).
  Downloaded unchanged the first time a song's piano is transcribed with
  ByteDance Piano Transcription, and checked against its SHA-256.
- YourMT3+ weights (the YPTF.MoE+Multi model without pitch-shift
  augmentation, logs/2024/mc13_256_g4_all_v7_mt3f_sqr_rms_moe_wf4_n8k2_silu_
  rope_rp_b36_nops/checkpoints/last.ckpt, by Sungkyun Chang, Emmanouil
  Benetos, Holger Kirchhoff and Simon Dixon). License: Apache-2.0, as the
  model card declares. Source: https://huggingface.co/mimbres/YourMT3 at
  revision e45ebd70398682d54b7bb1901a5216e18f3b1824. Downloaded the first time
  a stem is transcribed with YourMT3+ and checked against its SHA-256, then
  converted on this computer into a weights-only file (the same tensors,
  checked against their own SHA-256); the downloaded file is then deleted.
